# Terms of Use

**Application:** Spending Tracker (Family Budget Tracker)

**Maintainer:** [falconsag](https://github.com/falconsag)

**Last updated:** 16 October 2026

## Personal use

Spending Tracker is a personal Python application for retrieving and analysing information from the maintainer's own or jointly held bank accounts for household budgeting. It is not offered as a public or commercial service.

Only accounts that the maintainer is entitled to access may be connected, with all required account-holder permissions. Use must comply with the connected bank's conditions and [Enable Banking's Terms of Service](https://enablebanking.com/terms/), including restrictions applicable to personal account access.

## Scope and responsibility

The application's intended function is to read account information and analyse spending. It is not intended to initiate payments, move money or provide financial, investment, tax or legal advice.

The user is responsible for securing their device, private keys, session credentials and downloaded records. Bank access may be withdrawn through the applicable bank or provider controls. Locally stored data must be deleted separately when no longer needed.

## Availability and accuracy

The application is provided as is, without a guarantee of availability, accuracy or fitness for a particular purpose. Bank and API availability, authorization expiry and data delays may affect results. Verify important figures against official bank statements before relying on them.

## Privacy and contact

Account information is handled as described in the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md). Questions may be directed to the maintainer's contact email registered for this application in the Enable Banking Control Panel. Do not disclose financial information or credentials in public GitHub issues.
